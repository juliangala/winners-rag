# Universidad de Salamanca (USAL) — Grado en Medicina

## Overview

The Universidad de Salamanca (USAL), founded in 1218, is the **oldest university in Spain and one of the oldest in the world**. This historic standing is not merely symbolic — it reflects over eight centuries of continuous academic tradition, making USAL a unique institution within the Spanish higher education landscape. The Facultad de Medicina delivers a 6-year, 360 ECTS Grado en Medicina that has undergone significant modernization, most notably receiving **WFME (World Federation for Medical Education) accreditation in June 2024** — an internationally recognized quality mark that allows graduates to apply for specialization and medical practice in the United States and Canada. Clinical training is conducted in collaboration with the Gerencia Regional de Salud de Castilla y León (SACYL) through teaching hospitals and primary care centres in Salamanca and across the region.

## Program Options

USAL offers a single Grado en Medicina on the Campus Miguel de Unamuno in Salamanca, taught exclusively in Spanish. Two plans of study currently coexist:

- **Plan 2022** — new plan, implemented progressively from 2022-23; applicable to all students enrolling from 2022-23 onward. This is the current plan for new entrants.
- **Plan 2010** — original plan, still active for students who began before 2022-23; being phased out progressively.

Both plans lead to the same degree. Students enrolling in 2026-27 will follow the **Plan 2022**.

## Program Structure

The Plan 2022 follows the national Orden ECI/332/2008 framework and organizes the degree into five modules plus an optional component:

1. Morphology, structure and function of the human body
2. Social medicine, communication skills, and research initiation
3. Human clinical training
4. Diagnostic and therapeutic procedures
5. Supervised practice and Final Year Project (TFG)
6. Optional module: specialties, surgical specialties, research in Medicine

| Year | Key Content Areas |
|---|---|
| Year 1–2 | Basic sciences: Anatomy, Biochemistry, Physiology, Histology, Cell Biology, Genetics, Immunology |
| Year 3 | Pathological Anatomy, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Psychiatry, Surgery Fundamentals |
| Year 3 (partial) | Diagnostic procedures practicum (6 ECTS — first clinical practical block) |
| Year 4–5 | Clinical medicine: Cardiology, Respiratory, Neurology, Ophthalmology, ENT, Dermatology, Paediatrics, Obstetrics, Gynaecology, Preventive Medicine, Legal Medicine |
| Year 6 | Full rotatory clinical practicums in primary and specialist care + ECOE assessment + Final Year Project (TFG) |

**Clinical practicum structure:** 60 ECTS total (6 ECTS in Year 3 — diagnostic procedures; 54 ECTS in Year 6 — full rotatory practicum in primary care and specialist settings). Year 6 concludes with an **ECOE (Evaluación de Competencias de Objetivos Específicos)** clinical competency assessment.

The degree is nationally recognized at **MECES Level 3**, the equivalent of a Master's degree within the Spanish higher education qualification framework.

## Teaching Methodology

USAL Medicine employs a methodology aligned with the national medicine competency framework. Teaching combines theoretical lectures, practical laboratory work (anatomy, histology, physiology, biochemistry), seminars, case-based and problem-based elements, and progressive clinical integration. The Facultad de Medicina operates dedicated practical laboratories and simulation resources. The university's digital campus (Studium, Moodle-based) supports course delivery. Language preparation for medical bibliography is embedded through recommended subject preparation in scientific English.

The ECOE at the end of Year 6 assesses clinical competencies acquired through rotatory practicums using a structured, standardized format — ensuring graduates meet defined clinical performance thresholds before receiving the degree.

## Clinical Training

Clinical training is conducted through the Concierto Específico between USAL and the Gerencia Regional de Salud de Castilla y León (SACYL). Practicums are carried out in primary and specialist care centres located predominantly in:

- **Salamanca** — principal location for most clinical rotations; university hospitals and primary care centres
- **Zamora** — additional specialist rotations
- **Ávila** — additional specialist rotations
- **León** — additional specialist rotations

Principal teaching hospitals include:

- **Hospital Universitario de Salamanca (Complejo Asistencial Universitario de Salamanca)** — major university hospital; primary clinical teaching centre
- **Hospital Universitario de Zamora**, **Hospital Universitario de Ávila**, **Hospital Universitario de León** — secondary rotation sites within the SACYL agreement

The Year 6 full rotatory practicum covers primary care (Atención Primaria) and specialist settings across all major clinical areas, assessed via the ECOE.

## International Recognition and Opportunities

**Key distinguishing feature:** The USAL Grado en Medicina obtained **WFME (World Federation for Medical Education) accreditation on 28 June 2024**. This accreditation:

- Allows USAL Medicine graduates to apply for medical specialization (residency) and to practice medicine in the **United States and Canada** from 2024 onward
- Is managed in Spain by ANECA on behalf of WFME
- Validity extends for four years from the announcement date

In addition to the EU-wide recognition under Bologna and Directive 2005/36/EC, the WFME accreditation significantly expands the geographic scope of recognized practice for USAL graduates — unique among Spanish public universities at the time of this accreditation.

Students may participate in:

- **Erasmus+** — bilateral clinical and academic placements at European partner universities
- **SICUE** — national inter-university exchange within Spain
- **Estudiantes Internacionales program** — USAL maintains a dedicated portal (estudiantesinternacionales.usal.es)
- **IFMSA** — international student medical exchange
- **Bilateral agreements** — Latin American and selected non-European institutions (including USAL's own international office, which historically manages a large international student population)

USAL's historic reputation and its Universidad de Salamanca brand (globally recognized in the Spanish-speaking world) give it particular international standing, especially in Latin America.

## Admission Model

Access to Grado en Medicina at USAL is governed by the Castilla y León autonomous community admissions system, with the nota de admisión calculated using the national formula.

**Nota de admisión formula:**

```
Nota de admisión = 0.6 × Nota media Bachillerato
                 + 0.4 × EVAU fase general score
                 + best 2 specific-phase subjects × ponderation coefficient
```

- Base maximum: **10.0000**
- Maximum with bonus: **14.0000**

## Admission Process

**Flow 1 — Spanish students (EVAU/PAU):**

1. Complete Bachillerato and pass the EVAU fase general in Castilla y León (or another region, with inter-regional recognition).
2. Optionally sit specific-phase subjects to maximize the nota de admisión.
3. Apply through the Castilla y León admissions system (usal.es/acceso-grados) during the application window.
4. List preferences in order.
5. Await adjudication — ordinary round lists published in July; follow-up lists through July/August.
6. Accept the offer and complete enrollment.
7. Complete online registration (automatrícula).

**Flow 2 — International students (PCE/UNED):**

1. Obtain Credencial de Acceso from UNED via PCE exams or EU credential route.
2. Ensure B2 Spanish proficiency documentation is available.
3. Apply through the Castilla y León admissions system for the international/foreign-student phase.
4. Provide certified and apostilled academic documents.
5. Await adjudication and complete enrollment.

## MIR Preparation and Professional Outcomes

The Grado en Medicina qualifies graduates to sit the **MIR** national examination for access to medical specialization in Spain.

- **MIR pass rates for USAL graduates:** Available via USAL quality indicators (see usal.es/grado-en-medicina, Indicadores de calidad section) and published through ACSUCyL evaluations
- **WFME accreditation** (June 2024): USAL graduates may apply for specialist training and medical practice in the **United States and Canada** — a distinctive and unique feature among Spanish public medicine programs at the time of this writing
- The ECOE at the end of Year 6 provides a structured clinical competency validation
- USAL's research infrastructure (bioscience research groups, Parque Científico, international partnerships) supports students with research career interests

Professional pathways include MIR specialization, general practice, SACYL employment in Castilla y León, biomedical research, and — via WFME accreditation — international medical practice in the US, Canada, and EU.

5. **SACYL clinical network geographic scope:** Clinical practicums extend across Salamanca, Zamora, Ávila, and León. Students should be prepared for potential rotations outside the city of Salamanca.

**Q: Is USAL really Spain's oldest university?**
A: Yes. Founded in 1218, the Universidad de Salamanca is the oldest continuously operating university in Spain and one of the oldest in the world. This historic standing is reflected in the university's architectural patrimony (the historic Plaza Mayor campus), international reputation, and the symbolic weight associated with an USAL degree — particularly in Latin American academic and professional contexts.
