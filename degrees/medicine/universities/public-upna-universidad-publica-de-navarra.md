# Universidad Pública de Navarra (UPNA) — Grado en Medicina

## Overview

The Universidad Pública de Navarra (UPNA), founded in 1987, is the public university of the Foral Community of Navarra. Its Grado en Medicina is offered through the Facultad de Ciencias de la Salud on the Campus de Salud in Pamplona. The programme is a relatively recent addition to UPNA's academic offering, reflecting the expansion of public Medicine provision in Navarra alongside the region's strategic commitment to health sciences education. Clinical training is centred on the Hospital Universitario de Navarra (HUN), the main tertiary referral hospital in the region and the primary teaching hospital for the programme. UPNA is a member of the **UNITA (Universities of Technology and Innovation Alliance)** European university alliance, supporting international academic collaboration and mobility. Admissions are governed by the nota de admisión through the Navarra university admissions system — no proprietary entrance exam, interview, or language test is required. Tuition is set under Gobierno de Navarra pricing regulation.

## Program Options

UPNA offers a single Grado en Medicina delivered primarily in Spanish. The institutional environment is bilingual (Spanish and Basque/Euskera), and some administrative and teaching elements may be available in Euskera, though the Medicine programme is conducted primarily in Spanish. There are no parallel English-medium tracks, no online or hybrid delivery modes, and no binational double-degree programmes within this degree.

## Program Structure

The programme follows the national framework established by Orden ECI/332/2008, structured across six academic years totalling 360 ECTS:

| Year | Key Content Areas |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | Human Anatomy, Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, Biostatistics, Cell Biology and Genetics, Physiology I, Introduction to Medicine |
| Year 2 | Histology and Embryology, Medical Physiology II, Microbiology, Immunology, Pharmacology I, Medical Communication and Bioethics |
| Year 3 | Pathological Anatomy, General Medicine I, General Surgery I, Pharmacology II, Psychiatry, Medical Imaging |
| Year 4 | General Medicine II, Paediatrics I, Obstetrics and Gynaecology I, Ophthalmology, ENT, Dermatology |
| Year 5 | Neurology, Traumatology and Orthopaedics, Urology, Preventive Medicine and Public Health, Legal Medicine, Primary Care |
| Year 6 | Full Clinical Rotations: Internal Medicine, Surgery, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Psychiatry, Emergency Medicine, Primary Care + Final Year Project (TFG, 6 ECTS) |

## Teaching Methodology

The programme combines lecture-based instruction in the preclinical years with increasing integration of problem-based learning (PBL), clinical simulation, and standardised patient scenarios. The Facultad de Ciencias de la Salud operates a Unidad de Educación en Ciencias de la Salud dedicated to developing and improving teaching methodology in health sciences. A body and brain donation programme supports anatomical dissection training from early years. From Year 3, students progressively integrate into the Hospital Universitario de Navarra under supervised clinical conditions. Clinical simulation infrastructure supports procedural skills development before direct patient contact.

## Clinical Training

Clinical training at UPNA is delivered through the Sistema Sanitario de Navarra (SNS-O, Servicio Navarro de Salud — Osasunbidea) under formal agreement between UPNA and the Gobierno de Navarra:

- **Hospital Universitario de Navarra (HUN)** — main tertiary referral and teaching hospital in Pamplona; the primary clinical training site, providing exposure across all major medical and surgical specialties
- **Complejo Hospitalario de Navarra** — supplementary placements across various Navarra health facilities (subject to annual confirmation)
- **Centros de Salud (Primary Care)** — Navarra Health Service primary care centres across the Pamplona area; Year 6 Primary Care rotation

The Hospital Universitario de Navarra provides students with access to a full-spectrum regional referral hospital environment, including emergency medicine, complex surgery, oncology, and medical specialties. All rotations are supervised and assessed by hospital clinicians and faculty staff.

## International Recognition and Opportunities

The UPNA Grado en Medicina is fully recognised across the EU under the Bologna Process and EU Directive 2005/36/EC on professional qualifications recognition. Graduates may apply for medical registration in any EU member state following national procedures. Extra-EU recognition is subject to bilateral agreements or local homologation.

UPNA is a member of the **UNITA European University Alliance**, supporting collaborative academic programmes and mobility. Mobility opportunities include:

- **Erasmus+** — clinical and academic placements at European partner institutions
- **UNITA Alliance** — collaborative programmes with universities in France, Italy, Portugal, Romania, and other European countries
- **SICUE** — national inter-university exchange programme within Spain
- **IFMSA** — student-led international medical exchange network
- **Bilateral agreements** — Latin American and non-European partner institutions

The Facultad de Ciencias de la Salud maintains specific mobility agreements for Health Sciences students, including dedicated clinical exchange programmes.

## Admission Model

Access to Grado en Medicina at UPNA is managed through the Navarra regional admissions system, coordinated by the Gobierno de Navarra and UPNA.

**Nota de admisión formula:**

```
Nota de admisión = 0.6 × Nota media Bachillerato
                 + 0.4 × EVAU fase general score
                 + best 2 specific-phase subjects × ponderation coefficient
```

- Base maximum (without bonus): **10.0000**
- Maximum with specific-phase bonus: **14.0000**
- No university-specific minimum grade beyond the national EVAU pass requirement (≥5.0)

**Ponderation coefficients for Medicina at UPNA (Navarra)** — set annually by the Navarra admissions commission and published by the Gobierno de Navarra:

| Subject | Coefficient |
|---|---|
| Biology | 0.2 # ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED |
| Chemistry | 0.2 # ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED |
| Physics | 0.2 # ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED |
| Mathematics II | 0.2 # ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED |

The best 2 eligible subjects are automatically selected to maximise the applicant's score. Students should confirm current ponderations at the official UPNA/Navarra admissions portal before sitting specific-phase subjects.

**Inter-regional applicants:** Students who sat their EVAU in a region other than Navarra may apply to UPNA Medicine. The nota de admisión formula is universal; however, applicants should verify that their specific EVAU subjects and regional ponderations are recognised in the Navarra admissions system.

**International students (PCE/UNED):** Students with international secondary qualifications must obtain a Credencial de Acceso through UNED via the PCE exams. The nota de admisión formula is identical. Students from EU systems may apply via the Credencial UE route. Selected-country homologation holders may apply directly.

## Admission Process

**Flow 1 — Spanish students (EVAU/PAU):**

1. Complete Bachillerato (2 years) and pass the EVAU fase general (EvAU Navarra or equivalent regional variant with inter-regional recognition).
2. Optionally sit specific-phase subjects to earn up to +4.0 bonus points on the nota de admisión.
3. Apply through the UPNA/Navarra admissions portal during the application window after EVAU results are published.
4. List degree programmes and campuses in order of preference (advisable to include multiple options as contingency).
5. Await adjudication results and follow-up lists through the waiting-list process.
6. Accept the offer and complete enrolment at UPNA within the stipulated deadline.
7. Complete online registration (automatrícula) by the deadline.

**Flow 2 — International students (PCE/UNED):**

1. Complete secondary education in the home country.
2. Obtain homologation of the secondary certificate OR sit PCE exams through UNED (available in Spain and at selected international centres).
3. Obtain the Credencial de Acceso from UNED.
4. Apply through the UPNA/Navarra admissions process for foreign students (separate phase with its own calendar, typically running before the domestic window).
5. Provide certified and apostilled academic documents.
6. Await adjudication and complete enrolment at UPNA.

## Risks and Considerations

1. **Programme maturity:** UPNA's Grado en Medicina is a relatively recent programme. MIR outcome data and long-term track record are still accumulating. Verify ANECA accreditation status and programme review results before applying.
2. **Nota de corte trajectory:** As the programme gains visibility nationally, the nota de corte may increase in coming years. Students should not assume current figures will remain stable.
3. **Navarra regional quota mechanics:** Navarra's admissions system may allocate seats differently from larger centralised systems such as DUA Andalucía. Confirm exact quota breakdowns and reservation seats with UPNA.
4. **Seat count variation:** Annual changes in offered seats directly affect the nota de corte. Monitor the UPNA catalogue each year.
5. **PCE/UNED international pathway complexity:** International students must complete PCE exams in the right subjects and obtain their Credencial in time for the UPNA foreign-student application window.
6. **Academic intensity:** Medicine at UPNA is a demanding programme; the workload from Year 1 requires consistent engagement. Official permanence norms apply.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How is the nota de admisión calculated for UPNA Medicine?**
A: Nota de admisión = 0.6 × Bachillerato average + 0.4 × EVAU fase general score + up to 2 specific-phase subjects × ponderation coefficient (currently 0.2 for Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics II at UPNA). Maximum possible score: 14.0000. No additional university exam or interview applies.

**Q: What is the nota de corte and why does it change each year?**
A: The nota de corte is the score of the last student admitted in the ordinary round. It is not set in advance; it results from the interaction of available seats, applicant volume, and score distribution in that specific year. For a newer programme like UPNA Medicine, the nota de corte may be particularly subject to variation as national awareness of the programme grows.

**Q: Can international students (PCE/UNED) apply to UPNA Medicine?**
A: Yes. International students must obtain a Credencial de Acceso through UNED — either via PCE exams (available at international centres) or EU credential recognition. They apply through the UPNA foreign-student admissions process. The nota de admisión formula and nota de corte are identical to domestic applicants.

**Q: What is the annual tuition fee for Medicine at UPNA?**
A: Approximately 1,283.00 EUR per year for first-enrolment students (source: notasdecorte.es / Gobierno de Navarra decree), subject to annual revision. This is somewhat higher than Andalusian public Medicine programmes but significantly lower than private Spanish medicine options.

**Q: When should I apply to UPNA Medicine?**
A: The UPNA domestic application window opens shortly after EvAU Navarra ordinaria results are published in late June. International students must apply through the UPNA foreign-student process, which has an earlier calendar. Always verify exact dates at unavarra.es and the Gobierno de Navarra admissions portal.

**Q: What ponderation coefficients apply to Biology, Chemistry, and Physics for UPNA Medicine?**
A: For Medicine at UPNA, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics II carry a coefficient of 0.2 in recent years. Confirm current values with UPNA or the Gobierno de Navarra before each EvAU sitting, as they are set annually.

**Q: Is UPNA's Grado en Medicina a new programme?**
A: UPNA's Medicine degree is a more recent programme compared to long-established faculties such as UGR or Sevilla. It has full ANECA accreditation and is a Bologna-compliant degree. Students should verify the current accreditation status and programme reviews at the time of application.

**Q: What is the primary teaching hospital for UPNA Medicine?**
A: The Hospital Universitario de Navarra (HUN) in Pamplona — the main tertiary referral hospital of the Navarra health system, covering all major medical and surgical specialties. Clinical rotations begin from Year 3 onward.
