# Universidad de Sevilla (US) — Grado en Medicina

## Overview

The Universidad de Sevilla (US), one of Spain's oldest and largest universities with origins in the sixteenth century, is the principal public research university of Andalucía's largest city. The Facultad de Medicina, located in the historic Macarena district adjacent to the Hospital Universitario Virgen Macarena, offers a 6-year, 360 ECTS Grado en Medicina recognized across the European Union. Admission is coordinated through the Distrito Único Andaluz (DUA) system and is determined exclusively by the nota de admisión — no proprietary exam, interview, or additional test is required. The US is home to approximately 70,000 students across all faculties, making it one of Spain's largest university institutions, with a correspondingly broad research and clinical teaching infrastructure in Sevilla.

## Program Options

The US offers a single Grado en Medicina taught exclusively in Spanish. There are no parallel tracks in other languages, no online or hybrid delivery formats, and no binational double-degree options within this degree.

## Program Structure

The program follows the national framework established by Orden ECI/332/2008 and is organized across six academic years for 360 ECTS:

| Year | Key Content Areas |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | Human Anatomy, Biochemistry, Biostatistics, Cell Biology & Genetics, General Physiology, Histology |
| Year 2 | Medical Physiology, Anatomy (applied), Medical Communication, Bioethics & Research Methods, Immunology |
| Year 3 | Pathological Anatomy, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Surgery Fundamentals, Psychiatry, Internal Medicine Foundations |
| Year 4 | Cardiology, Respiratory Medicine, Neurology, Ophthalmology, ENT, Dermatology, Radiology, Urology |
| Year 5 | Preventive Medicine & Public Health, Obstetrics, Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Endocrinology, Legal Medicine, Clinical Pharmacology |
| Year 6 | Full clinical rotations across all major specialties + Final Year Project (TFG) |

The program includes a compulsory Final Year Project (Trabajo Fin de Grado) completed in Year 6.

## Teaching Methodology

Teaching at US Medicine combines traditional lecture-based instruction with seminars, practical laboratory sessions, and progressive clinical integration from the mid-years onward. Clinical simulation and patient communication training are incorporated in the curriculum. From Year 3, students undertake supervised hospital-based practical sessions. Year 6 is dedicated almost entirely to full-time clinical rotations across the teaching hospital network. The University of Sevilla operates its own virtual teaching platform (Enseñanza Virtual) used for resource distribution and hybrid support across all subjects.

## Clinical Training

Clinical training is conducted through the Servicio Andaluz de Salud (SAS) hospital network under the framework agreement between the US and the Junta de Andalucía:

- **Hospital Universitario Virgen Macarena** — major university hospital, directly adjacent to the Facultad de Medicina; primary teaching venue
- **Hospital Universitario Virgen del Rocío** — large tertiary referral centre and university hospital in Sevilla; second major teaching hospital
- **Hospital Universitario Virgen de Valme** — university hospital; supplementary teaching rotations
- **Centros de Salud (Primary Care)** — SAS primary care centres in the Sevilla health area; Year 6 Primary Care rotation

This dual large-hospital system offers students exposure to a very wide clinical caseload, including tertiary-level complex cases. Year 6 rotations cover Internal Medicine, Surgery, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Psychiatry, Emergency Medicine, and Primary Care.

## International Recognition and Opportunities

The US Grado en Medicina is recognized across the EU under the Bologna Process and EU Directive 2005/36/EC on professional qualification recognition. Graduates may apply for medical registration in any EU member state. Extra-EU recognition depends on bilateral agreements or home-country homologation procedures.

Students may participate in:

- **Erasmus+ exchange** — clinical and academic placements at European partner institutions
- **SICUE** — national inter-university exchange within Spain
- **Bilateral agreements** — partnerships with Latin American and selected non-European institutions
- **IFMSA** — international student medical exchange network

The US is a large university with an established international office and a sizeable incoming Erasmus student population, contributing to an internationally diverse campus environment.

## Admission Model

Access to Grado en Medicina at the US is governed by the **Distrito Único Andaluz (DUA)** system, identical in structure to all Andalucían public universities.

**Nota de admisión formula:**

```
Nota de admisión = 0.6 × Nota media Bachillerato
                 + 0.4 × EVAU fase general score
                 + best 2 specific-phase subjects × ponderation coefficient
```

- Base maximum: **10.0000**
- Maximum with bonus: **14.0000**

**Ponderation coefficients for Medicina at US (Andalucía)** — set annually by the Comisión del Distrito Único Universitario de Andalucía:

Coefficients are published annually in the BOJA. Students should verify current values before sitting specific-phase subjects.

**International students (PCE/UNED):** Same process as all Andalucían public universities. Students must obtain a Credencial de Acceso through UNED via PCE exams or EU credential recognition. The nota de admisión formula is identical to domestic applicants.

## Admission Process

**Flow 1 — Spanish students (EVAU/PAU):**

1. Complete Bachillerato and pass the EVAU fase general in Andalucía (inter-regional recognition applies).
2. Optionally sit specific-phase subjects to maximize the nota de admisión.
3. Apply through the DUA portal (juntadeandalucia.es/sguit) after EVAU results are published.
4. List program and university preferences in order (include multiple options as contingency).
5. Await adjudication — initial list published mid-July; follow-up lists through October.
6. Accept the offer through the DUA portal and enroll at the US admissions service.
7. Complete online registration (automatrícula).

**Flow 2 — International students (PCE/UNED):**

1. Complete secondary education in the home country.
2. Obtain homologation of the secondary certificate OR sit PCE exams through UNED.
3. Obtain the Credencial de Acceso from UNED.
4. Apply through the DUA foreign-student admission process (separate earlier calendar).
5. Provide certified and apostilled academic documentation.
6. Await adjudication and complete enrollment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How is the nota de admisión calculated for US Medicine?**
A: Nota de admisión = 0.6 × Bachillerato average + 0.4 × EVAU fase general score + up to 2 specific-phase subjects × ponderation coefficient. For Medicine in Andalucía, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics II currently carry a coefficient of 0.2 (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED). Maximum possible: 14.0000. No additional exam or interview.

**Q: What is the nota de corte?**
A: The nota de corte is the score of the last student admitted in the ordinary round of a given year. It is determined retroactively and published by the DUA after all waiting-list movements. It should be treated as an orientation reference, not a guaranteed threshold for future years.

**Q: Can international students (PCE/UNED) apply to US Medicine?**
A: Yes. International students must obtain a Credencial de Acceso through UNED (via PCE exams or EU credential recognition) and apply through the DUA foreign-student admission process on an earlier calendar than domestic students.

**Q: What is the annual tuition fee?**
A: Approximately 1,000.00–1,300.00 EUR per year for first-enrolment students (subject to annual revision by the Junta de Andalucía).

**Q: When should I apply to US Medicine?**
A: The DUA domestic application window opens after EVAU ordinaria results in late June. International students should consult the DUA foreign-student calendar, which opens significantly earlier (typically February–March). Verify at juntadeandalucia.es/sguit annually.

**Q: What are the ponderation coefficients for UGR/US — are they the same?**
A: Yes. All Andalucían public universities share the same ponderation table set by the Comisión del Distrito Único Universitario de Andalucía. For Medicine, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics II currently carry 0.2 (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED). Always confirm in the BOJA before your EVAU.
