# Universidad del País Vasco / Euskal Herriko Unibertsitatea

## Overview

The Universidad del País Vasco / Euskal Herriko Unibertsitatea (UPV/EHU) is the main public university of the Basque Country and one of Spain's largest universities, with campuses across the three Basque provinces. Its Grado en Medicina is offered at the Facultad de Medicina y Enfermería on the Leioa campus (Bizkaia), a short distance from Bilbao. The programme is notably unique in the Spanish public system: Medicine can be studied in Spanish, in Euskera (Basque), or with elements of both, and English is also listed as a teaching language component. With 200 seats and a nota de corte of 12.554 (2025-26 convocatoria ordinaria), UPV/EHU Medicine occupies a distinct competitive tier — significantly below Andalucía's elite programmes — making it strategically accessible to a broader candidate population without sacrificing prestige or public pricing. Clinical training is delivered within the Osakidetza (Basque Health Service) hospital network, including four University Hospitals across the Basque provinces.

## Program Options

The Grado en Medicina at UPV/EHU is offered in two linguistic streams:
- **Spanish (Castellano) stream:** Instruction primarily in Spanish, with some content in English.
- **Basque (Euskera) stream:** Instruction primarily in Euskera, with clinical rotations conducted at Hospital Universitario de Galdakao (specifically noted for the Basque-language group).

Both streams follow the same 360-ECTS, six-year curriculum and lead to the same degree. Students select their preferred language group at matriculation. The Basque-language stream is limited to students with sufficient Euskera proficiency. The programme does not offer a full English-medium track, though English is integrated as a teaching element. There are no dual degree options within Medicine at UGR/EHU level.

## Program Structure

The Grado en Medicina at UPV/EHU is structured across six years following the national ECI/332/2008 order:

- **Years 1–2 (Basic Sciences):** Foundational biomedical sciences — morphology, anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, histology, genetics, immunology, and biostatistics. Communication skills and the human sciences of medicine are introduced early.
- **Years 3 (Intermediate Year):** Transition to clinical integration — microbiology, pharmacology, pathophysiology, and introductory clinical skills.
- **Years 4–6 (Clinical Training — at University Hospitals):** The last three years are conducted at one of the University Hospital units (Basurto, Cruces, Galdakao for Basque-stream students, Araba for Spanish-stream students, and Donostia in San Sebastián). Rotations cover internal medicine, surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, paediatrics, psychiatry, emergency medicine, primary care, and elective specialties. A Trabajo Fin de Grado (TFG) is completed in Year 6. Upon graduation, the degree confers a Máster-equivalent level (Level 3 MECES), enabling direct doctoral programme access.

Virtual hospital facilities at the faculty are used for simulation-based clinical training prior to the hospital rotations.

## Teaching Methodology

UPV/EHU Medicine is noted for strong simulation infrastructure, including virtual hospital facilities within the Leioa campus where students perform clinical simulations on virtual patients before entering real clinical environments. This early clinical simulation exposure is a methodological differentiator. Teaching blends lecture-based instruction, case-based learning, and practical skills labs. The availability of dual-language instruction (Spanish and Euskera) is a distinctive regional feature. The faculty actively promotes research integration and has direct links to Osakidetza's hospital research units, providing students with access to biomedical research environments during clinical years.

## Clinical Training

UPV/EHU Medicine's clinical network is managed by Osakidetza (the Basque Health Service) and comprises five University Hospital units:

- **Hospital Universitario de Basurto** (Bilbao — primary clinical unit for Spanish-stream Years 4–6)
- **Hospital Universitario de Cruces** (Barakaldo, near Bilbao — one of the largest hospitals in northern Spain)
- **Hospital Universitario de Galdakao** (Galdakao — Basque-stream clinical unit)
- **Hospital Universitario de Araba** (Vitoria-Gasteiz — Spanish-stream clinical unit)
- **Hospital Universitario Donostia** (San Sebastián / Donostia — clinical unit for Gipuzkoa)

Students in the Spanish-stream are assigned to either Basurto, Araba, or Donostia; Basque-stream students are primarily at Galdakao. The multi-provincial clinical structure means that some students conduct their final three years in cities other than Leioa/Bilbao (specifically Vitoria-Gasteiz or San Sebastián). This geographic diversity exposes students to different clinical environments but requires accommodation and lifestyle flexibility. Virtual hospital simulations at Leioa supplement hospital-based learning.

## Admission Model

UPV/EHU Medicine uses the standard Spanish public university nota de admisión model. Admission is governed by the País Vasco regional admissions system (not the Andalucía DUA), with ponderation coefficients set independently by the Basque Country's inter-university admissions commission.

**Formula:**
```
Nota de admisión = 0.6 × Nota media Bachillerato + 0.4 × Nota EVAU fase general
                  + (Subject A score × coefficient) + (Subject B score × coefficient)
```

- Base maximum: **10.0000**
- Maximum with specific-phase bonus: **14.0000**
- Ponderation coefficients for Medicine at UPV/EHU are set by the País Vasco regional admissions system and may differ from Andalucía coefficients (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED — verify annually with the UPV/EHU admissions service).
- Biology and Chemistry are expected to carry coefficients of 0.2 for Medicine (consistent with national practice), but this must be confirmed each cycle.

**International students (PCE/UNED):** Non-EU students may access the programme via PCE/UNED. EU students with a recognised credential apply via the standard Credencial UE pathway. Basque Country admissions are managed separately from the Andalucía DUA system.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How is the nota de admisión calculated?**
Formula: 0.6 × Bachillerato average + 0.4 × EVAU general phase + up to two specific-phase bonuses (score × ponderation coefficient). Ponderation coefficients are set by the País Vasco regional admissions commission — verify annually. Biology and Chemistry are expected to carry 0.2, but confirm before the cycle.

**What is the nota de corte?**
The nota de admisión of the last candidate admitted in the final waiting-list round. For 2025-26, it was 12.554 (convocatoria ordinaria), per the official UPV/EHU degree page.

**Can Medicine be studied in Basque at UPV/EHU?**
Yes. UPV/EHU is the only Spanish public university offering a full Basque-medium (Euskera) Medicine programme. Students in the Basque stream conduct clinical years primarily at Hospital Universitario de Galdakao.

**Can international students apply via PCE/UNED?**
Yes. Non-EU students may obtain a nota de acceso via PCE/UNED. EU students with a recognised secondary credential apply through the Credencial UE pathway to the País Vasco regional admissions system.

**What are the tuition fees?**
Approximately 18.92 EUR per credit for first matriculation (2025-26), resulting in approximately 1,135 EUR/year for 60 credits. Total degree cost approximately 6,811 EUR over six years (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED).

**When do I apply?**
Applications are submitted through the País Vasco regional admissions system in June/July (following the EVAU ordinaria). Exact dates are published annually (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED).

**Where do students spend their clinical years?**
Clinical years (Years 4–6) are conducted at University Hospital units across the Basque Country: Basurto and Araba (Spanish stream), Galdakao (Basque stream), and Donostia (San Sebastián). Students may be required to relocate to these cities for their clinical years.

**Is there an entrance exam or interview?**
