# Universidad Alfonso X el Sabio — Campus Oviedo: Medicine Degree

## Programme Overview

The Universidad Alfonso X el Sabio (UAX) opened the first private Medicine faculty in Asturias in September 2026, at its new affiliated centre — Centro Universitario Alfonso X el Sabio Asturias — in Oviedo. This makes UAX Oviedo a historic addition to the Asturian healthcare training landscape: prior to 2026, Medicine in the region was only available through the University of Oviedo (public). The UAX Oviedo campus is a centro adscrito (affiliated centre) authorised under Decreto 110/2025 of the Principado de Asturias, operating within the UAX academic framework.

The degree is the official Grado en Medicina (360 ECTS, 6 years), governed by Orden ECI/332/2008 and fully recognised for access to the MIR examination and EU-wide professional recognition. The programme curriculum at Oviedo mirrors the UAX framework established at the Madrid campus: the same subject structure, the same UAXmakers educational model, and the same integrated Digital Health subject in Year 3. As of the 2026-27 academic year, the plan de estudios is formally described as "in process of modification" for delivery at the affiliated centre, meaning structural adjustments specific to the Oviedo context may apply for the first cohort.

UAX positions its Medicine offering on three institutional pillars: a 96% MIR pass rate (published across all its graduates, primarily from the Madrid campus), early Digital Medicine integration as a formal Year 3 subject, and a clinical network spanning public and private hospitals. Students at Oviedo access an Asturian clinical network covering more than 38 specialties — a key advantage for students who wish to train and potentially build a career in the north of Spain.

The campus includes classrooms, laboratories, a cafeteria, a common room, and an auditorium. Facility images currently shown on UAX's website are described as digital renders and may be subject to modification — prospective students should request an in-person visit to verify current state. Students at Oviedo do not have on-campus access to the Virtual Simulation Hospital (>2,000 m²) located at the Madrid campus in Villanueva de la Cañada; whether equivalent simulation infrastructure is available at the Oviedo centre is PENDING_CONFIRMATION.

## Location and Campus

The UAX Oviedo campus operates as Centro Universitario Alfonso X el Sabio Asturias, located in Oviedo, the regional capital of the Principado de Asturias. Oviedo is a mid-sized city of approximately 220,000 inhabitants, with a strong public healthcare infrastructure anchored by the Hospital Universitario Central de Asturias (HUCA) and a network of public and private hospitals distributed across the region.

The exact campus address has not been publicly confirmed. The city itself is compact and navigable on foot and by public transport, with significantly lower cost of living than Madrid — private room rentals typically range from approximately 300.00 to 500.00 EUR per month in the city centre.

Oviedo offers a distinctive quality-of-life profile for Medicine students: a manageable city size, close proximity to the Asturian coast and mountains, and a robust public hospital ecosystem for clinical rotations. The regional health service, SESPA (Servicio de Salud del Principado de Asturias), covers a broad range of specialties across Asturias, making the regional clinical network a substantive training environment.

## Admission Process

For the 2026-27 cycle, the date of the admission exam convocatoria at UAX Oviedo has not been published yet. The Madrid campus holds its Medicine-specific admission test on a continuous basis, but the 2026-27 exam calendar does not list continuous sittings for Oviedo. For a new campus receiving its first cohort in 2026-27, earlier applications carry a meaningful strategic advantage — seat numbers are limited and undisclosed.

Admission to the Grado en Medicina at UAX Oviedo proceeds through the following stages:

1. **First contact and eligibility check.** Applicants contact UAX (telephone, WhatsApp, or online form) and an advisor guides them through the admission process. Eligibility requires a Bachillerato title and PAU pass (or accredited equivalent). International credentials must be evaluated.

2. **Admission test fee payment.** A 125.00 EUR fee is payable before sitting the Medicine-specific exam. Payment instructions are provided by the UAX admissions team.

3. **Prueba Específica de Medicina (Part 1 — Written Exam).** A 50-question multiple-choice test in two sections: Biology (30 questions) and Chemistry (20 questions), based on the standard Bachillerato curriculum. The exam assesses scientific aptitude relevant to medical study.

4. **Prueba Específica de Medicina (Part 2 — Personal Interview).** A personal interview conducted as the second phase of the admission test. The relative weight of the interview in the final admission decision and its specific format at the Oviedo campus are PENDING_CONFIRMATION.

5. **Admission decision.** UAX evaluates the combined test and interview performance. The scoring formula and minimum required score are not published.

6. **Place reservation and enrolment.** Admitted students pay the reserva de plaza (3,000.00 EUR) and apertura de expediente (1,350.00 EUR), then complete administrative enrolment.

Applicants without B2-level Spanish must enrol in UAX's Spanish language accompaniment programme: 2,400.00 EUR in Year 1, and 800.00 EUR in Years 2 and 3.

An alternative entry route — the Programa de Acceso a Medicina — is also available: students who complete all subjects in this UAX preparatory programme receive a guaranteed place in the Medicine degree, bypassing the standard exam. See the UAX website for cost and conditions.

The Becas de Excelencia UAX explicitly exclude the Grado en Medicina. Students should not plan financial aid around this scholarship. Available options for UAX Oviedo Medicine students include:

The UAX Oviedo website lists "Comunidad de Madrid" in its scholarships section — this appears to be a copy of the Madrid page and should be disregarded by Asturian students; the relevant authority is the Consejería de Educación del Principado de Asturias.

- **Ayuda Deportistas de Élite:** 20–50% discount on docencia for students holding UAX elite athlete classification.
- **Continuidad de Estudios UAX:** Up to 25% discount for students with a prior UAX qualification.
- **Pronto Pago:** 2% reduction for full upfront annual payment. Compatible with other discounts.
- **Familiares de Estudiantes UAX:** 5% discount for direct family members (up to second degree) of enrolled UAX students.

Contact admisiones@uax.es or +34 91 910 01 70 for details on applicable aid.

## Clinical Training

UAX Oviedo's clinical network is distributed across public and private hospitals throughout Asturias. The programme page lists the following named partner institutions: Hospital Vital Álvarez Buylla (Mieres), Clínica Asturias, Hospital Begoña, Hospital Gijón, and Hospital Covadonga — the latter being part of the HUCA (Hospital Universitario Central de Asturias) complex in Oviedo. UAX states that additional centres beyond those named are also included in the network.

Clinical rotations cover more than 38 medical and surgical specialties. The programme page for Oviedo cites 1,600 practical hours — a figure distinct from the 1,800 hours stated for the Madrid campus. Students should confirm the final practical hours figure directly with UAX Oviedo admissions, as the curriculum plan is still formally undergoing modification for delivery at this centre.

Clinical exposure at patient level begins in Year 4 with Clínica Práctica I (6 ECTS). Years 5 and 6 are the most clinically intensive: Clínica Práctica II (12 ECTS, Year 5) and Clínica Práctica III (36 ECTS, Year 6) — the latter constituting the principal clinical immersion year. Year 3 introduces procedural and simulation-based training through Procedimientos Médico-Quirúrgicos Generales (18 ECTS), conducted prior to hospital placement.

Because UAX Oviedo is a new campus commencing its first cohort in 2026-27, the finality of clinical centre agreements, confirmed rotation slots, and the volume of student placements per hospital remain PENDING_CONFIRMATION. **What makes UAX Oviedo's Medicine programme distinctive?**
UAX Oviedo is the first private university to offer a Medicine degree in Asturias. Students train within an Asturian clinical network spanning public and private hospitals, making it the only option for those who wish to pursue private-sector Medicine education without leaving the region. The programme shares the same UAX curriculum, admission model, Digital Health integration, and MIR preparation framework as the established Madrid campus.

**Is this a new programme — what does that mean for students considering the 2026-27 cohort?**
Yes. The 2026-27 intake at UAX Oviedo is the first cohort ever admitted. This means there is no historical track record of MIR pass rates, graduation rates, or clinical rotation outcomes specific to this campus. The curriculum plan is formally described as "in process of modification" for delivery at the Oviedo affiliated centre. Prospective students should treat this as a new programme launch and verify the finalized plan de estudios, clinical centre agreements, and facility readiness directly with UAX before committing.

**Is the admission process the same as for the Madrid campus?**
Partly. The test is the same: a 50-question MCQ test in Biology (30 questions) and Chemistry (20 questions), plus a personal interview. The exam fee is 125.00 EUR. The calendar differs: the Madrid campus holds the test on a continuous basis, while the date of the 2026-27 convocatoria for Oviedo has not been published yet. Seat availability is limited and not disclosed — early application is advisable.

**What hospitals will I train in?**
UAX lists the following Asturian clinical partners: Hospital Vital Álvarez Buylla (Mieres), Clínica Asturias, Hospital Begoña, Hospital Gijón, and Hospital Covadonga (part of the HUCA complex), along with additional centres. No single primary teaching hospital is officially designated. The finalised hospital agreement list and confirmed rotation capacity should be verified directly with UAX before enrolment, as the campus is new for 2026-27.

**What is the annual tuition at UAX Oviedo?**
The tuition for UAX Medicine is 24,250.00 EUR per year (2026-27, ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED). This figure is based on the published Madrid campus price, as UAX does not list a separate fee for Oviedo. Confirm the Oviedo-specific price directly with UAX admissions. In addition to tuition, a reserva de plaza of 3,000.00 EUR and apertura de expediente of 1,350.00 EUR are payable. Tuition may be paid in 10 monthly instalments of 1,990.00 EUR.

**Are there scholarships available?**
The main UAX Becas de Excelencia explicitly exclude Medicine. Available options include MEC national scholarships, Principado de Asturias excellence scholarships, the OEI 50% discount for Iberoamerican nationals, UAX elite athlete discounts (20–50%), and the continuidad de estudios discount (up to 25%). Contact admisiones@uax.es for the current list.

**Can I study Medicine at UAX Oviedo if I studied outside Spain?**
Yes, UAX accepts international curricula and accepts students via equivalent access credentials. A B2-level Spanish language certificate is required for non-native Spanish speakers, or enrolment in UAX's Spanish language accompaniment programme (2,400.00 EUR in Year 1; 800.00 EUR in Years 2 and 3). Contact UAX admissions to confirm the credential equivalency process for your specific educational background.
