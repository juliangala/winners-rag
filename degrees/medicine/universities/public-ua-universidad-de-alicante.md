# Universidad de Alicante (UA) — Grado en Medicina

## Overview

The Universidad de Alicante (UA) is a public university located in San Vicente del Raspeig, on the northern outskirts of Alicante, within the Comunitat Valenciana. Founded in 1979, UA is principally known for its programmes in Law, Economics, Business, and Social Sciences. The Grado en Medicina is a relatively recent addition to UA's academic portfolio — the faculty launched the Medicine degree approximately around 2021-2022 — and is housed within the Facultad de Ciencias de la Salud.

For the 2025-26 intake, UA Medicine recorded a nota de corte of 12.718 (source: notasdecorte.es), placing it in the medium-high competitiveness tier among Spanish public Medicine programmes. This is notably lower than the major Valencian university (UV — Universidad de Valencia), making UA an accessible alternative for well-prepared candidates seeking a public Medicine programme in the Mediterranean region.

Admission is managed through the CGPAU (Comissió Gestora de Processos Universitaris), the Comunitat Valenciana's centralised admissions authority. There is no proprietary entrance exam and no interview.

## Program Options

UA offers a single Grado en Medicina track at the San Vicente del Raspeig campus. There is no bilingual cohort, no separate English-medium group, and no alternative campus for Medicine. All admitted students follow the same curriculum and hospital rotation network.

As a newly established Medicine faculty, UA is still building out the full infrastructure of its programme. Candidates considering UA Medicine should be aware that the programme is younger than those of major Spanish medical faculties, which may affect the availability of historical data, established MIR track records, and some programme elements.

## Program Structure

The Grado en Medicina at UA is structured across 6 academic years totalling 360 ECTS, following the Spanish national framework for Medicine degrees.

**Phase 1 — Preclínica (Years 1–2):** Core biomedical sciences taught at the UA campus in San Vicente del Raspeig. Subjects include Human Anatomy, Biochemistry, Physiology, Cell Biology, Histology, Microbiology, Immunology, Pharmacology, and Pathology. Clinical skills training is integrated from early stages.

**Phase 2 — Clínica (Years 3–6):** Hospital-based clinical training in affiliated teaching hospitals. Students rotate through internal medicine, surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, paediatrics, psychiatry, primary care, and other specialties. Year 6 includes a dedicated clinical rotation period.

**TFG (Final Degree Project):** Mandatory. Language and format requirements PENDING_CONFIRMATION.

**Electives:** Optional subjects integrated across the degree plan (exact ECTS and subject list PENDING_CONFIRMATION).

## Admission Model

UA Medicine participates in Spain's public university admissions system, administered through the CGPAU (Comissió Gestora de Processos Universitaris) — the Comunitat Valenciana's centralised admissions authority. Admission is determined exclusively by the nota de admisión. There is no proprietary entrance exam and no interview.

**The nota de admisión formula:**

```
Nota de admisión = 0.6 × Nota media de Bachillerato
                 + 0.4 × Nota EVAU (fase general)
                 + up to 4.0 bonus points from EVAU fase específica
```

- **Base score maximum:** 10.0000
- **Maximum nota de admisión:** 14.0000 (with full specific-phase bonus)
- **Specific phase:** Best 2 eligible subjects selected. Each subject grade × ponderation coefficient (0.1 or 0.2) set annually by CGPAU.

## Admission Process

### For Spanish PAU students

1. Complete Bachillerato and sit EVAU (Comunitat Valenciana PAU or equivalent in other regions).
2. Receive nota de admisión from the EVAU authority.
3. Submit preinscripción through the CGPAU portal (accesuniversitats.gva.es) during the designated window (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED).
4. List UA Medicina among your degree preferences in the Comunitat Valenciana system.
5. Await results of the ordinaria convocatoria.
6. If not admitted in the ordinaria: apply for the extraordinaria convocatoria or pursue alternatives.
7. If admitted: complete enrolment (matrícula) at UA within the published deadline.

### For international PCE/UNED students

1. Obtain a secondary qualification equivalent to Spanish Bachillerato.
2. Apply to UNEDasiss for a Credencial. Submit transcripts and request subject recognition.
3. Sit PCE at UNED for subjects relevant to Medicine — Biology and Chemistry are typically most strategically valuable (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED — verify CGPAU coefficients).
4. Receive final UNEDasiss Credencial with calificación (5–10 scale).
5. Submit preinscripción through the CGPAU portal (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED for exact process and dates).
6. If admitted: complete enrolment and arrange visa/NIE if applicable.

**How is the nota de admisión calculated?**
The nota de admisión = 0.6 × Nota media de Bachillerato + 0.4 × Nota EVAU (fase general) + bonus from up to 2 specific-phase subjects (each subject grade × ponderation coefficient of 0.1 or 0.2). The maximum score is 14.0000.

**What is the nota de corte for UA Medicine?**
For the 2025-26 intake, the nota de corte was 12.718 (source: notasdecorte.es). It is not a fixed threshold — it changes annually. As a newer programme, historical nota de corte data for prior cycles is PENDING_CONFIRMATION.

**When do I apply?**
Applications (preinscripción) are submitted through the CGPAU portal in June–July for the ordinaria convocatoria. An extraordinaria convocatoria follows in August–September. All dates are ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED — verify at accesuniversitats.gva.es.

**Is Valencian required?**
No. Valencian (a variant of Catalan) is co-official in the region but is not an admission requirement for UA Medicine. Primary instruction is in Spanish.

**Is there an interview or entrance exam?**
No. UA Medicine admission is based solely on the nota de admisión within the CGPAU framework. There is no interview, proprietary exam, or portfolio requirement.

**When does clinical training begin?**
From Year 3. Years 1–2 are taught at the UA campus in San Vicente del Raspeig. From Year 3, students rotate through affiliated hospitals, primarily the Hospital General Universitario de Alicante — Dr. Balmis.
