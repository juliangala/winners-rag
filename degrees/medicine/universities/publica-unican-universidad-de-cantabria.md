# Universidad de Cantabria

## Overview

The Universidad de Cantabria (UNICAN) is a public research university designated as a Campus de Excelencia Internacional, with its Facultad de Medicina located in Santander. The Grado en Medicina is a six-year, 360-ECTS programme delivered in Spanish, governed by Cantabria's regional university admissions process.

A defining institutional milestone occurred in July 2025, when UNICAN's Medicine faculty obtained the **Sello Internacional de Calidad (SIC)** from the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME), awarded for an eight-year period (23 July 2025 to 22 July 2033). This certification, managed by ANECA, confirms that UNICAN's Medicine programme meets international standards for medical education quality. Crucially, this qualification enables UNICAN graduates to apply for ECFMG (Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates) certification, which is a prerequisite for practicing medicine in the United States and Canada.

UNICAN is also part of the **EUNICE European University Alliance**, reflecting its commitment to European academic cooperation. Admission is governed by the nota de admisión formula; there is no proprietary exam or interview. The primary teaching hospital is the prestigious Hospital Universitario "Marqués de Valdecilla" in Santander.

## Program Options

UNICAN offers a single Medicine programme — the Grado en Medicina — delivered on the Santander campus. There is no bilingual track, no distance-learning option, and no separate parallel programme. The Facultad de Medicina also offers a Grado en Ciencias Biomédicas (a distinct degree), but Medicine itself is a standalone programme.

Students from any Spanish autonomous community may apply through Cantabria's university preinscripción process. International students access through the PCE/UNED or Credencial UE pathways.

A new plan de estudios was introduced for students beginning from the 2024-25 academic year, with updated subject guides for those starting their studies from that point onward. Students who began prior to 2024-25 follow a different plan de estudios.

## Program Structure

The Grado en Medicina at UNICAN is structured over 6 years (360 ECTS). The programme's structure emphasises clinical training as a central element:

- **Years 1–2:** Foundational biomedical sciences (Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Histology, Genetics, Cell Biology, Immunology, Microbiology)
- **Years 3–5:** Clinical disciplines (Internal Medicine, Surgery, Obstetrics, Paediatrics, Psychiatry, Pharmacology, Neurology, Radiology, and clinical rotations progressively integrated)
- **Year 6 (Rotatorio Clínico):** Full-year supervised clinical rotation at Hospital Universitario "Marqués de Valdecilla" and affiliated centres; covers all major clinical specialities

The programme includes a Trabajo Fin de Grado (TFG), completed during Year 6. The university publishes separate subject listings and teaching guides for students entering from 2024-25 and for those who began earlier.

## Teaching Methodology

UNICAN's Medicine programme combines classroom-based theoretical instruction with strong early exposure to clinical and laboratory environments. The proximity of the Facultad de Medicina to Hospital Universitario "Marqués de Valdecilla" — one of northern Spain's leading tertiary hospitals — enables integrated clinical teaching from the early middle years of the degree.

The Faculty has designated clinical practice and surgical skills facilities, supplemented by laboratory infrastructure for biomedical sciences instruction. The Campus de Excelencia Internacional designation reflects UNICAN's research intensity, which translates into access to research groups and research-linked teaching activities within the Facultad.

The EUNICE European University network provides additional academic enrichment and cross-border learning opportunities for motivated students.

## Clinical Training

UNICAN's clinical training is anchored at:

- **Hospital Universitario "Marqués de Valdecilla"** (Santander) — primary teaching hospital; one of the largest and most specialised tertiary care hospitals in northern Spain. Home to numerous clinical departments, including oncology, cardiology, neurology, haematology, and surgery.
- **Additional concertado (affiliated) centres** — the university references other concerted healthcare centres for specific rotation requirements (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED — exact current list should be confirmed with the Facultad).
- **Primary care centres (Centros de Salud)** — for family medicine rotations.

The rotatorio clínico in Year 6 constitutes a significant portion of the degree's credit load and covers all major clinical areas. UNICAN describes clinical practices as "a defining feature of the degree" (seña de identidad de la titulación).

## International Recognition and Opportunities

UNICAN holds the **WFME Sello Internacional de Calidad (SIC)**, obtained on 24 July 2025, valid until 22 July 2033. This is among the most significant international accreditations available to a medical faculty, as it:

1. Confirms compliance with internationally recognised medical education quality standards.
2. Enables graduates (including those who graduated from 10 June 2024 onward) to apply for **ECFMG certification**, allowing them to pursue medical residency training and practice in the **United States and Canada**.
3. Facilitates EU employer recognition and provides assurance to employers of graduates' clinical and academic competencies.

The degree is also recognised under EU Directive 2005/36/EC, ensuring automatic professional recognition across all EU member states.

UNICAN is a member of the **EUNICE European University Alliance**, linking it with partner universities across Europe for collaborative academic and mobility programmes.

Erasmus+ mobility: UNICAN participates in Erasmus+. Medicine-specific partner universities and available mobility slots must be confirmed with the Facultad de Medicina annually (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED).

## Admission Model

Admission to Medicine at UNICAN is managed through Cantabria's university admissions process (preinscripción). UNICAN publishes historical nota de corte data in its statistical documentation (Evolución Notas de Corte).

**The nota de admisión formula:**

```
Nota de admisión = (0.6 × Nota media Bachillerato) + (0.4 × EVAU general phase grade)
                 + (best 2 specific-phase subjects × ponderation coefficient)
```

- Base score maximum: 10.0000 points
- Specific-phase bonus maximum: 4.0000 additional points
- Absolute maximum nota de admisión: 14.0000

**Subject ponderation coefficients at UNICAN for Medicine (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED):**

| Subject | Coefficient |
|---|---|
| Biology (Biología) | 0.2 |
| Chemistry (Química) | 0.2 |
| Physics (Física) | 0.1 |
| Mathematics II | 0.1 |

Candidates should sit Biology and Chemistry in the EVAU specific phase to maximise their nota de admisión for UNICAN Medicine.

**International students (PCE/UNED pathway):** International candidates access UNICAN through PCE/UNED, generating a Credencial UNED that functions as an equivalent nota de admisión. The same ponderation coefficients apply. EU candidates may also use the Credencial UE pathway.

## Admission Process

### For Spanish EVAU (PAU) Candidates

1. Complete Bachillerato and sit the EVAU (any region's variant is accepted).
2. Sit the EVAU specific phase — prioritise Biology and Chemistry.
3. Calculate your nota de admisión using UNICAN's ponderation table.
4. Access Cantabria's university preinscripción portal during the official application window (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED) and submit ranked preferences.
5. Await provisional and definitive assignment results from the Cantabrian admissions authority.
6. Confirm and formalise enrolment at the Facultad de Medicina in Santander.

### For International Students (PCE/UNED Pathway)

1. Verify your secondary qualification's eligibility for PCE/UNED access.
2. Register with UNED and sit the PCE examinations (typically May–June — ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED).
3. Obtain the Credencial UNED as your nota de admisión equivalent.
4. Apply through Cantabria's preinscripción system with the Credencial UNED.
5. If admitted, arrange student visa (non-EU) and complete enrolment documentation.

For students interested in practicing medicine in the US or Canada post-graduation, UNICAN's WFME accreditation opens the ECFMG pathway — an exceptional differentiator among Spanish public universities.

- Students with international professional ambitions — particularly those considering practicing medicine in the US or Canada — for whom UNICAN's WFME accreditation is a structural advantage over non-accredited programmes.

### Less Suitable For

- Candidates whose nota de admisión falls below 13.55 — historical data suggests access is not viable.
- Students who do not need WFME/ECFMG recognition and for whom the nota de corte of UNICAN exceeds their realistic performance — other comparable public programmes may be more accessible.

- Candidates who require a bilingual or English-medium track — UNICAN offers Medicine in Spanish only.

## Risks and Considerations

**Nota de corte increase risk:** The WFME accreditation obtained in July 2025 is likely to increase UNICAN's attractiveness to high-performing candidates in subsequent cycles, potentially pushing the nota de corte above 13.70 in 2025-26 and beyond. Candidates targeting UNICAN should plan for a higher threshold than the 2024-25 figure suggests.

**Small cohort:** With approximately 100 seats, UNICAN has limited capacity. Any increase in demand relative to supply exerts upward pressure on the nota de corte.

**Extraordinary call:** Residual seats in the September extraordinary call are typically very limited for Medicine. Do not plan admissions strategy around this pathway.

**Plan de estudios split:** Students beginning from 2024-25 follow a new curriculum plan. Candidates should verify the current plan's structure and available electives at time of application.

**ECFMG timeline:** The ECFMG pathway requires additional steps beyond graduating from UNICAN (passing USMLE exams, residency matching). The WFME accreditation is a necessary but not sufficient condition for US/Canada practice; it removes one barrier but does not guarantee employment.
