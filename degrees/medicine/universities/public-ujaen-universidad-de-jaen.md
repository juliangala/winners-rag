# Universidad de Jaén (UJAEN) — Grado en Medicina

## Overview

The Universidad de Jaén (UJAEN) is a public university founded in 1993, serving the inland province of Jaén in Andalucía. Its Grado en Medicina is one of several recently established medical programmes at Spanish public universities, launched approximately in 2020-2021 as part of Andalucía's expansion of public medical education capacity. The programme operates from the Facultad de Ciencias de la Salud on the Campus Las Lagunillas in Jaén.

Admission is governed by the **Distrito Único Andaluz (DUA)**, the centralised admissions system of the Junta de Andalucía. There is no proprietary entrance exam, no interview, and no language test requirement. The admissions process is purely score-driven, based on the nota de admisión calculated from Bachillerato grades and EVAU performance.

The principal affiliated teaching hospital is the Hospital Universitario de Jaén, and clinical placements may also occur within the broader Complejo Hospitalario de Jaén network. As a newer programme in a smaller Andalusian city, UJAEN's Medicine faculty is still building its clinical infrastructure and accumulating MIR track record data.

The nota de corte for Medicine at UJAEN in 2025-2026 was 13.066, placing it in the medium-high competitiveness band and making it one of the more accessible public medical schools in Spain by score threshold.

## Program Options

UJAEN offers a single Grado en Medicina programme, delivered entirely in Spanish on the main campus in Jaén. There is no bilingual track, no English-medium option, no distance-learning variant, and no parallel international programme. All students follow a unified six-year curriculum.

The programme is accessible via the DUA portal (Junta de Andalucía) for Spanish EVAU candidates and via PCE/UNED or Credencial UE for international students. UJAEN's Medicine programme is part of the recent wave of Andalusian public universities expanding their health sciences offerings, alongside Almería (UAL) and Huelva (UHU).

## Program Structure

The Grado en Medicina at UJAEN follows the standard Spanish public medical degree structure across 6 years (360 ECTS):

| Year | Main focus |
|---|---|
| Year 1 | Foundational biomedical sciences: anatomy, physiology, biochemistry, cell biology, histology |
| Year 2 | Pathophysiology, pharmacology, microbiology, immunology, genetics |
| Year 3 | Introduction to clinical medicine; first supervised hospital placements begin |
| Year 4 | Core clinical disciplines: internal medicine, surgery, paediatrics, obstetrics and gynaecology |
| Year 5 | Advanced clinical rotations across medical and surgical specialities |
| Year 6 | Rotatorio clínico (full-year supervised clinical rotation across departments and primary care) + TFG |

Each academic year comprises approximately 60 ECTS. The Year 6 rotatorio clínico is the programme's primary applied clinical component.

## Teaching Methodology

UJAEN applies the standard Spanish public medical school teaching model: theoretical instruction via lectures and seminars, laboratory-based practical work, clinical skills simulation, and progressively increasing direct patient contact from Year 3 onward. Problem-based learning elements and research methodology are integrated from the early years of the curriculum.

As a newer faculty in a mid-sized inland city, UJAEN's teaching infrastructure and pedagogical refinement are still developing. The university has historical strengths in environmental sciences and engineering, and its health sciences faculty builds on these research capabilities where applicable to biomedical science foundations.

## Admission Model

Admission to Medicine at UJAEN is governed by the **Distrito Único Andaluz (DUA)**, the centralised admissions system of the Junta de Andalucía, managed by the **Comisión del Distrito Único Universitario de Andalucía**. Candidates apply through the regional portal and rank their preferred degree-university combinations. There is no direct application to UJAEN.

**The nota de admisión formula:**

```
Nota de admisión = (0.6 × Nota media Bachillerato) + (0.4 × EVAU general phase grade)
                 + (best 2 specific-phase subjects × ponderation coefficient)
```

- Base score maximum: 10.0000 points
- Specific-phase bonus maximum: 4.0000 additional points
- Absolute maximum nota de admisión: 14.0000

**Subject ponderation coefficients for Medicine at UJAEN / Andalucía (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED):**

| Subject | Coefficient |
|---|---|
| Biology (Biología) | 0.2 (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED) |
| Chemistry (Química) | 0.2 (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED) |
| Physics (Física) | 0.2 (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED) |
| Mathematics II | 0.2 (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED) |
| Other | ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED |

In Andalucía, the four main science subjects carry equal 0.2 ponderation coefficients for Medicine. This means that Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Mathematics II each contribute identically to the specific-phase bonus. Candidates should sit whichever two of these four subjects they can achieve the highest scores in, rather than prioritising a specific pair on coefficient grounds.

**International students (PCE/UNED pathway):** Students with secondary education completed outside Spain may access UJAEN via PCE/UNED. Alternatively, EU secondary school graduates may present a Credencial UE for direct equivalence assessment.

## Admission Process

### For Spanish EVAU (PAU) Candidates

1. Complete Bachillerato and register for the EVAU in your region.
2. Sit both the general phase and, strategically, the two specific-phase subjects in which you can achieve the highest scores (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Mathematics II — all 0.2 coefficient in Andalucía; ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED).
3. Calculate your estimated nota de admisión.
4. During the DUA application window (ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED), register on the Junta de Andalucía admissions portal and rank university-degree combinations.
5. Await provisional allocation and confirm your place within the stated deadline.
6. Complete enrolment at UJAEN's Facultad de Ciencias de la Salud.

### For International Students (PCE/UNED Pathway)

1. Verify your secondary school qualification is eligible for PCE/UNED access.
2. Register with UNED and sit the PCE examinations (typically May/June — ANNUAL_UPDATE_REQUIRED).
3. Request your Credencial UNED, converting PCE results into a nota de admisión equivalent.
4. Participate in the DUA process through the Junta de Andalucía portal.
5. If accepted, obtain a student visa (non-EU applicants) and complete all enrolment documentation at UJAEN.

Non-EU international students must also verify general Spanish university access requirements with the Spanish Ministry of Education prior to applying.

The MIR (Médico Interno Residente) is the national competitive exam required to access specialist training in Spain. All Spanish Medicine graduates must sit the MIR. UJAEN-specific MIR pass rates are PENDING_CONFIRMATION as the first cohorts are still progressing through the programme.

**Is Jaén a good city for students?**
Jaén is a smaller inland Andalusian city with a low cost of living, a compact campus environment, and proximity to natural landscapes. Students accustomed to large metropolitan areas should research Jaén's urban context before committing to six years in the city.
